Aircraft Checklist Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Aircraft Checklist is a flight-simulation and training-reference application. It is not intended for real-aircraft operation.

Information We Collect

Aircraft Checklist does not require user accounts and does not ask users to provide personal information.

The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information through the app.

Local App Data

Checklist progress and related app preferences may be stored locally on the user's device so the app can remember the user's progress. This locally stored information is not transmitted to the developer.

Advertising and Tracking

Aircraft Checklist does not contain advertising and does not use advertising identifiers for ad targeting.

Analytics

The app does not use developer-operated analytics to track users across apps or websites.

Third-Party Platforms

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may independently process technical or account information as part of providing the Google Play service, subject to Google's own privacy policies. This processing is controlled by Google and is separate from data collection by the Aircraft Checklist app.

Children's Privacy

Aircraft Checklist is intended for adults and is not directed to children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be posted at this page.
