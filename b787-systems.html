Privacy Policy · Effective October 28, 2026

About this policy

This privacy policy applies to B787 Systems, the aircraft systems visual-reference app provided by Aero Utility Studio. It describes the standalone mobile app, this policy website, and optional support correspondence. It is separate from the policies for our other apps.

Using the reference library

The core app works offline. Reference diagrams are bundled with the app. You do not need an account. The app does not transmit your searches, favorite references or recently viewed references to Aero Utility Studio or an app backend.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the reference IDs you mark as favorites and an ordered list of your eight most recently viewed reference IDs. These are stored locally using React Native AsyncStorage. Search text, navigation and zoom state are held in memory while you use the app; they are not saved as a search history. The app does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, audio, advertising identifier or payment information during ordinary reference-library use. Your Light or Dark appearance preference is also saved locally and is not sent to us.

Analytics, tracking and third-party software

B787 Systems uses Expo, React Native and supporting libraries for the interface, bundled assets and local storage. The current app has no advertising, analytics, third-party crash-reporting, account, cloud-sync or over-the-air update service enabled. It does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and it does not sell or share your reference activity for advertising. Development tools used to create or preview the app are separate from the standalone app.

Permissions and device features

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photo library or advertising tracking. Its local reference data is stored in the app’s own storage area. Android includes the normal Internet permission; that permission does not itself send data. Opening a policy link or support email uses your browser or email application.

Retention, deletion and backups

Favorites remain on your device until you remove them using the star on a reference or remove the app’s local data. The recent list retains only the latest eight references. A confirmed recovery reset replaces an unreadable favorites list; it does not clear recent references. You can remove local app data by deleting the app, or on Android by clearing its storage in device settings. iOS offloading may retain app data. The current iOS storage library requests exclusion of favorites and recent history from iCloud backup. Android allows operating-system backup, so app data may be backed up or restored depending on your device and account settings. Manage existing backups through your device or backup provider. We cannot access or remotely delete the reference data stored only on your device.

Optional support email

If you choose to email meveyeart@gmail.com, we receive your email address, the contents of your message and any attachments you send. We use that information to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue you report. The app does not automatically attach your favorites, recent references or diagnostic logs. Messages are handled through email providers, including Gmail for our support mailbox, and remain in that mailbox until deleted. Contact the same address to request access, correction or deletion of your support correspondence. Do not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

This policy website and external services

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and processes website request information under its privacy statement. This policy page contains no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, embedded media or forms, and we do not set cookies on it. Your browser, email provider, app store and operating-system backup or diagnostic services may process information under their own policies and your settings. Opening an external link does not send your stored favorites or recent-reference list to that site.

Security and privacy choices

Local data is kept within the app’s storage area and relies on the protections of your device and operating system. AsyncStorage is not a separate encrypted vault. Protect your device and any backups. You can use the reference library without opening external links or contacting support. For questions about this policy or information you have sent us, email meveyeart@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version. Review the policy for the version of B787 Systems you use.
