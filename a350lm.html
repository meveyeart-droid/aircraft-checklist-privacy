A350 LM Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

A350 LM is an aviation maintenance training and reference application published by Aero Utility Studio. It is not intended to replace approved aircraft maintenance documentation or operator procedures.

Information We Collect

A350 LM does not require a user account and does not ask users to provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment information, or other contact information.

The app does not transmit user content or usage data to the developer and does not use a configured analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting service.

Local App Data

A350 LM may store notes, bookmarks, custom fault/reset records, acknowledgement state, backup settings, and related timestamps locally on the user's device. Free-text entries can contain information supplied by the user.

The app and its underlying framework may process limited technical information locally, such as screen dimensions, system interface information, and session or device metadata needed for normal app operation. No transmission of that information to the developer was identified in the audited app.

Files, Imports, and Backups

The app can access documents that a user explicitly selects. Selected files may be copied into the app's local cache for processing.

On supported Android devices, users can choose a folder for manual or automatic backup files. Those backups may contain custom entries, notes, bookmarks, record metadata, and timestamps. A350 LM does not upload those backups to a developer-controlled server.

Device operating-system backups, user-selected folders, or third-party document providers may synchronize files through services chosen or configured by the user. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and settings.

Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

A350 LM does not contain advertising and does not use advertising identifiers for ad targeting. The app does not use developer-operated analytics or tracking across apps or websites.

Device Permissions

The audited app does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, Bluetooth, motion sensors, or advertising tracking.

Data Security and Deletion

Local app records and exported backup files are not separately encrypted by A350 LM. Device and platform security protections may still apply.

Deleting an item in the app may not immediately remove copies that remain in local snapshots, caches, operating-system backups, user-created backup files, or third-party storage selected by the user.

App Stores and Third-Party Platforms

A350 LM may be distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Apple, Google, and user-selected storage providers may independently process technical, account, purchase, or service information under their own privacy policies. That processing is separate from data collection by A350 LM.

Children's Privacy

A350 LM is intended as a professional training and reference tool and is not directed to children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if A350 LM's features or data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
