B777 LM Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

B777 LM is an aviation maintenance reference and training application published by Aero Utility Studio. It does not replace current approved aircraft maintenance documentation or operator procedures.

Information and App Use

B777 LM does not require an account. The app does not send your searches, favorites, or personal Quick Fix entries to the developer. It has no configured advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party crash-reporting service.

Reference records and recognition figures are included with the app for offline use. Search text is used to filter that local content and is not saved as a search history.

Local Data and Recovery

The app saves favorite record identifiers and personal Quick Fix entries on your device. Personal entries can include a title or fault, status or message type, maintenance message, and action notes. Any personal information you type into these fields becomes part of your saved content.

After saving, the app attempts to update a recovery copy in its local storage. It can restore a valid recovery copy when a saved value is missing or damaged. Recovery copies are not a separate cloud service and do not protect against losing or clearing all app storage.

Clipboard, Exports, and Imports

Copy controls place the information you select on your device clipboard. The backup export control copies your personal Quick Fix entries as text. Favorites are not included in this manual export.

You choose whether to paste exported text into another app, file, or service. To import a backup, you paste its text into B777 LM, where it is checked and merged locally. The app does not upload these backups to a developer-operated server.

Your operating system or other apps may retain or synchronize clipboard content and exported files according to their own settings and privacy policies.

Device Backups

Operating-system backup or device-transfer services may include app data, depending on your device, account, and backup settings. Android app backup is enabled in B777 LM. The operating system controls backup timing, availability, and restoration; the app cannot confirm when a system backup last completed.

Retention, Deletion, and Security

Saved entries and favorites remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app's stored data. You can delete a personal Quick Fix entry using its edit controls and remove a favorite by tapping its star. The app attempts to update the local recovery copy after each change, including deletion; it reports a warning if that copy cannot be updated.

Clearing app storage or deleting the app normally removes its local data. Copies in device backups, clipboard history, or files you saved elsewhere may remain and must be managed through those services. A device restore can bring back an older backup.

B777 LM does not add separate encryption to saved entries or exported backup text. Device and operating-system protections still apply. Protect your device and keep any exported backups in a location you trust.

App Stores, Websites, and Support

Apple, Google, and your selected backup or storage providers may independently process account, purchase, diagnostic, or service information under their own privacy policies. This is separate from app data being sent to the developer.

Opening this online policy uses your browser. GitHub Pages hosts the policy and may process ordinary website request information, such as an IP address, under GitHub's privacy statement.

If you contact support by email, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to send. That correspondence is used to answer your request and retained only as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence at the address below.

Children's Privacy

B777 LM is intended as a professional reference and training tool and is not directed to children.

Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The updated policy and its date will be posted on this page.
